# Privacy Policy for Jehad Al-Muslim (جهاد المسلم)

**Last Updated: May 11, 2026**

This privacy policy is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
For a better experience, while using our Service, we do not require you to provide us with any personally identifiable information. The app is designed to respect your privacy.

* **Internet Access:** The app requires internet access to fetch Prayer Times, Quranic texts, and audio files.
* **Local Storage (DOM Storage):** We use local storage on your device only to save your Tasbeeh count and app settings so they are not lost when you close the app.
* **Location Data:** The app determines prayer times based on your IP address or manual selection. We **DO NOT** use or request precise GPS location permissions.

### 2. Log Data
We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app, we may collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data.

### 3. Cookies
This Service does not use these “cookies” explicitly.

### 4. Service Providers
We may employ third-party companies and individuals for the following reasons:
* To provide data for Prayer Times (Aladhan API).
* To provide Quranic content (Al Quran Cloud API).

### 5. Security
We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable.

### 6. Children’s Privacy
These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

# سياسة الخصوصية لتطبيق جهاد المسلم

1. **جمع المعلومات:** التطبيق لا يطلب منك إدخال أي بيانات شخصية (مثل الاسم أو رقم الهاتف).
2. **صلاحيات الإنترنت:** يحتاج التطبيق للاتصال بالإنترنت فقط لجلب مواقيت الصلاة ونصوص القرآن والأصوات.

4. **التخزين المحلي:** نستخدم ذاكرة الهاتف فقط لحفظ عداد السبحة الخاص بك لكي لا يضيع عند إغلاق التطبيق.
